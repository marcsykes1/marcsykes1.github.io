What brokerages want to know before they switch

The migration plan normally covers the records needed to keep operating: agents and users, active deals, commission plans, year-to-date and commission-year figures, agreed historical deal data, and the financial opening position. The exact source depends on what the current systems can export.

They are prepared for import before cutover and checked in RealDesk. The old and new systems can run side by side during the transition so staff are not rebuilding live files on launch day.

Commission plans, year-to-date totals and commission-year progress are established before launch. RealDesk validates the imported figures against the supplied reports before launch.

RealDesk configures the chart of accounts, records the opening position and completes the initial reconciliations before launch. The objective is to begin in RealDesk from checked balances, not reconstruct the books after cutover.

Brokerage staff and conveyancers train on the full deal workflow. Bookkeepers and accountants train on accounting, reporting and reconciliation. Agents can receive direct training or be trained by the brokerage using a train-the-trainer model.

Yes. Both systems may operate temporarily during migration and cutover, but the normal end state is for transactions, commissions, payments, accounting and reporting to run in RealDesk.
